Exploring Malaysia’s Medical Landscape

This dashboard explores healthcare accessibility across Malaysia.

By analyzing hospital infrastructure alongside historical poverty trends, household income, and demographic diversity, it reveals the complex social factors that shape medical equity in Malaysian society.

Chapter 1: The History of Poverty Reduction (1970 - 2022)

Understanding today's healthcare disparities requires looking at Malaysia's long-term economic development and poverty alleviation history.

Insight

In 1970, the absolute poverty rate of most regions exceeded 40%, but it decreased dramatically over 50 years, indicating national economic growth.

However, some regions still face significant challenges, notably Sabah, which lags behind the peninsula's rapid development.

National Healthcare Budget Allocation
Insight

While the MOH budget has grown steadily since 2015, the most significant surge occurred post-COVID, with total spending jumping 27% in just two years to reach RM 41.2B by 2024.

Insight

Piped water access varies significantly across districts, with Bukit Mabong (21.6%) and Song (29.8%) critically underserved, while electricity coverage remains consistently high throughout East Malaysia.
